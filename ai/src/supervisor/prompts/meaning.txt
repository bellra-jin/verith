FIELD ROLES
- mentions: quote only raw stock/company names or explicitly supplied six-digit codes. Never put
  외국인, 기관, 개인, periods, amounts or perspective words here. Never choose a canonical
  company/code for an ambiguous name; literal 현대 remains 현대 for the server resolver.
- views: quote the minimal explicit perspective term. flow means trading flows (수급, 순매수,
  순매도, 자금흐름, 자금 흐름, flow); technical means chart analysis; fundamental means financial
  analysis. overview requires explicit broad/company-information wording, never a company name.
  Keep every requested/negated perspective, including unsupported or conflicting ones.
- conditions: preserve participants, focus/exclusion, periods and cutoffs separately. 외국인 말고
  is flow/exclude with value 외국인 and adjacent negation 말고, not a stock or negated Flow view.
  Preserve the exact number and original unit: 주 is not 일 or 거래일. A holding quantity such as
  100주 보유 중 is not evidence for a definite 100-week analysis period. Do not discard unrecognized
  wording to make a proposal pass. The server alone decides support, waivers and clarification.

EVIDENCE CONTRACT
Use exact continuous text occurring once in its named source. source_kind/answer_id select only
that original query or that exact typed answer. Never borrow matching words/negation from another
answer or the question. Use zero-based Python Unicode [start,end) offsets; the server derives
positions from unique exact text, never your numbers. No rewriting, trimming or Unicode repair.
Prefer view evidence 수급 and a separate condition 3주, rather than the broader 3주 수급.
The only composite alignment is one positive period recognized by the server's existing Flow
period parser, followed by spaces/tabs and one unique Flow term in the same exact source. The
server may narrow that view span while retaining the independently parsed unsupported period.
It will not erase participants, other views, negation, holding context or other leftover text.
Repeated/ambiguous words, multiple periods, mixed fields or unsupported composites are rejected.
A supplied negation must be its own exact adjacent span in the same source. Do not infer it or
move it between fields. Never change 기관 to 외국인, OFS to CFS, 거래일 to 일, or dates to latest.
Do not infer absent facts, consent, capabilities, owner fields, tools, canonical IDs or prose.
